Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your pricing rates? +
Local rides start at ₹15/km for 4-seater and ₹16/km for 6-seater. Outstation trips are ₹16/km and ₹17/km respectively. A minimum of 250 km/day applies for outstation. Driver allowance of ₹500/day is added. Toll & parking charged at actual.
Is a driver included with the car? +
Yes, all bookings include a professional, licensed driver. We do not offer self-drive. The driver allowance of ₹500/day is charged separately for outstation trips.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept cash and all major UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). No advance payment required for booking — just confirm via WhatsApp or call.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Cancellations made 12+ hours before pickup are free. Within 12 hours may incur a nominal fee. Please contact us on WhatsApp or phone to cancel.
Do you provide airport transfers? +
Yes! We provide Patna Airport pickup and drop 24/7. Our drivers track your flight and are always on time. Book at least 24 hours in advance for guaranteed availability.
