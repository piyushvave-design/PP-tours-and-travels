Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your pricing rates?
Local rides start at ₹15/km for 4-seater and ₹16/km for 6-seater. Outstation trips are ₹16/km and ₹17/km respectively. A minimum of 250 km/day applies for outstation bookings. Driver allowance of ₹500/day is added. Toll & parking are charged at actual.
Is a driver included with the car?
Yes, all our bookings include a professional, licensed driver. We do not provide self-drive options. The driver allowance of ₹500/day is charged separately for outstation trips.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept cash and all major UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). No advance payment required for booking — just confirm via WhatsApp or call.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 12+ hours before pickup are free of charge. Cancellations within 12 hours may incur a nominal fee. Please contact us on WhatsApp or phone to cancel.
Do you cover airport transfers?
Yes! We provide Patna Airport pickup and drop services 24/7. Our drivers track your flight and are always on time. Book at least 24 hours in advance for guaranteed availability.
